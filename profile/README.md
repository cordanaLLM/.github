## Where the projects go

The projects here are moving to new homes under new names:

- products and tools:
  [Tekmora](https://github.com/tekmora)
- shared libraries and frameworks:
  [Plurano](https://github.com/plurano)

Nothing is deleted. A repository that has moved redirects to its new address.
One that has not moved yet stays here until its first release under the new
name.
